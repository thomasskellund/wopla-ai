Domain 2 — Chat

1 item
Gate company–vendor room visibility on active/future orders
Legacy hides a company–vendor chat room from non-admin participants once there's no current-or-future order between that company and vendor (room + history stay intact, just hidden from the list). We shipped all four chat room types with unconditional visibility instead, since the ordering domain doesn't exist yet. Once it does, add the order-relationship filter to api.list_chat_rooms — the room/membership tables already have the right shape, this is a query change, not a migration.

Domain 3 — Ordering

7 items
The other 8 order modules (Meeting, Fruit, Guest, Kiosk, Special, Bakery, Home, Coffee)
First pass ships Lunch only. The engine (standing order, daily materialization, cutoff math) is designed generally enough that adding a module is a data/config change — new modules row plus dishes — not a rearchitecture. Fruit additionally needs its own Thursday-anchored grace-period variant, noted separately below.
ordering public.modules
Fruit's Thursday-anchored major-update grace variant
Legacy's major-update cutoff is Monday-anchored for Lunch but Thursday-anchored for Fruit. engine.can_make_major_update only implements the Lunch anchor this pass — needs a per-module anchor-weekday before Fruit can be added.
ordering engine.can_make_major_update blocked on: Fruit module
Commercial contract terms (pricing, kickback %, delivery terms, switch-requests)
public.orders ships as a minimal company–vendor–module link with a validity window and no commercial fields — deliberately, since pricing/kickback/ switch-request workflows are a distinct future contracts/billing domain, not part of the ordering engine itself.
ordering public.orders

Employee offboarding/reassignment order-cancellation logic
Legacy force-cancels a departing/reassigned employee's future daily choices as part of offboarding (with a real bug there we're not reproducing — a || that should be && wipes cancellation metadata off orders that are already locked or cancelled). No offboarding flow exists yet in wopla-ai at all, so there's nothing to hook this into this pass.

Domain 4 — Invoicing

5 items
Full commercial contracts (delivery pricing, per-dish approval-workflowed pricing, multi-module rates)
public.billing_rates ships as a deliberately minimal stand-in (per-head vendor/customer rate + kickback %, date-versioned) for exactly what Lunch invoicing needs. Legacy's real Contract/ContractOrder also covers delivery pricing and a time-versioned, approval-workflowed per-dish price table (DishUserPrice) for non-combined modules — neither is needed until a second module or per-dish billing exists.
invoicing public.billing_rates
Enforce minimum_heads as an actual billing floor
daily_orders.minimum_heads stays inert this pass, matching legacy's own real behavior — despite the schema and field name implying otherwise, legacy never substitutes it for actual heads anywhere in the invoice engine. Wiring it up for real would be a genuine improvement over legacy, not parity, and was deliberately deferred rather than assumed.
invoicing daily_orders.minimum_heads
Employee-share payroll export (Danløn/Datalon/Intega-style deduction file)
Research surfaced that daily_orders.employee_share doesn't belong to invoicing at all in legacy — it's consumed exclusively by a separate payroll-export feature that turns ordered-lunch counts into a payroll deduction file. Not touched this pass; needs its own domain.
invoicing daily_orders.employee_share needs its own domain
Multi-counterparty ("bulk") invoices
Legacy's Invoice↔InvoiceUser is one-to-many — one invoice can bill several companies or vendors at once. This pass flattens to exactly one counterparty per invoice as a deliberate simplification, not an oversight; widening later is schema-additive.
invoicing public.invoices
A real overdue status with reminders/escalation
This pass only computes an is_overdue flag at read time (status = sent, past due date) — legacy has no equivalent at all. A persisted status plus reminder/escalation emails is a bigger, separate feature deliberately not built here.
